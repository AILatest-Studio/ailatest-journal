# 知刊：学术期刊检索与投稿参考

AILatest Journal（知刊）是 AILatest 的期刊检索与投稿决策支持平台。科研人员可以在投稿前搜索期刊、比较分区和收录、查看预警信号，并整理候选清单。

AILatest Journal is AILatest’s journal search and submission decision-support platform. Researchers use it to discover venues, compare rankings and indexing, check warning signals, and keep a shortlist before they submit.

线上产品：[journal.ailatest.org](https://journal.ailatest.org)。工作室介绍：[ailatest.org/projects/journal/](https://ailatest.org/projects/journal/)。本文件是公开说明，实现细节在私有仓库。

Live product: [journal.ailatest.org](https://journal.ailatest.org). Studio page: [ailatest.org/projects/journal/](https://ailatest.org/projects/journal/). This file is public documentation. Implementation stays in a private repository.

## 检索与比较

按刊名、缩写、ISSN、学科、索引和分区筛选，把投稿前常用的信息放在同一条工作流里。

Filter by title, abbreviation, ISSN, subject, index, and ranking, and keep the pre-submission facts in one workflow.

公开索引与目录包括 SCIE、SSCI、AHCI、ESCI、EI、Scopus、DOAJ、MEDLINE，以及中国、印度、韩国、马来西亚等地区站。详情页展示 JCR 影响因子与 Quartile、中科院分区（含 TOP）、CCF、ABDC、ABS、OA / APC，以及有公开样本时的审稿周期。

Public indexes and directories include SCIE, SSCI, AHCI, ESCI, EI, Scopus, DOAJ, MEDLINE, and regional stations such as China, India, Korea, and Malaysia. A journal page shows JCR impact factor and quartile, CAS zones (including TOP), CCF, ABDC, ABS, OA / APC, and review-cycle notes when a public sample exists.

## 风险信号

预警视图覆盖中科院预警、中信所预警、WoS On Hold 和 Under Review。撤稿提示来自公开整理，只作为风险参考。

Warning views cover the CAS warning list, the CITIC warning list, WoS On Hold, and Under Review. Retraction cues come from public compilations and are risk signals.

## 荐刊、收藏与插件

荐刊接受论文标题、摘要或关键词，结合主题、学科、收录和分区生成候选期刊，并分成优先推荐和备选。它帮助缩小检索范围，不承诺录用。

Recommend takes a paper title, abstract, or keywords and returns candidate venues from topic, subject, indexing, and ranking signals, split into priority picks and alternatives. It narrows a search. It does not promise acceptance.

登录后可以使用云端收藏和多份清单。浏览器插件在 Google Scholar、PubMed、知网等页面显示分区与收录徽章。定价采用统一积分：期刊字段对登录用户开放，AI 荐刊等动作消耗积分。额度以[定价页](https://journal.ailatest.org/pricing)为准。

Signed-in accounts can use cloud favorites and multiple lists. The browser extension shows zone and indexing badges on Google Scholar, PubMed, and CNKI. Pricing is a shared credit balance: journal fields are available after sign-in, and actions such as AI matching spend credits. Quotas follow the [pricing page](https://journal.ailatest.org/pricing).

## 数据从哪里来

站点聚合 Web of Science、JCR、中科院分区、EI、Scopus、DOAJ、MEDLINE、ESI、OpenAlex、CCF、ABDC、ABS、科协目录和国内核心期刊目录等公开数据与元数据。各来源的发布周期不同，页面标注来源、版本和截止时间。完整对照表在[关于与数据说明](https://journal.ailatest.org/about)。

The site aggregates public data and metadata from Web of Science, JCR, CAS zones, EI, Scopus, DOAJ, MEDLINE, ESI, OpenAlex, CCF, ABDC, ABS, CAST lists, and domestic core-journal directories. Sources update on different schedules. Pages show the source, edition, and cutoff. The comparison table is on the [about page](https://journal.ailatest.org/about).

本仓库不提供这些名录的再分发。著作权归原发布机构。知刊与上述机构没有隶属或背书关系。

This repository does not redistribute those directories. Copyright stays with the original publishers. AILatest Journal is not affiliated with or endorsed by those organizations.
